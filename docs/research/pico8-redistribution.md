# PICO-8 games with redistribution permission

## Scope and answer

User decision: Korri will never be a commercial product. Noncommercial redistribution licenses therefore belong in the candidate list. The earlier four-game shortlist was not a count of eligible games, and its commercial-first recommendation did not match that decision.

This catalogue records 28 gameplay titles with published noncommercial redistribution grants. Thirteen were in the first candidate table, 13 are new discoveries after the user's selection, and two have specific asset questions recorded separately. Nine games are selected. This is a checked sample, not an exhaustive catalogue. The unrestricted-license list is separate.

This research verifies permission declarations, not gameplay or Korri compatibility. No game was installed, played, or added to a release. A declaration is not a warranty that the developer controls every third-party right.

## What Korri must retain

For CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 games, preserve supplied creator credits, copyright and license notices, warranty notices, and a link to the original material. Include the license text or link. Identify modifications and retain notices of earlier modifications. If Korri distributes an adaptation, meet ShareAlike for that adaptation. Do not impose restrictions or DRM that prevent recipients from using their licensed rights.

These duties come from the actual [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/legalcode.en), sections 2 and 3. Section 2 grants the right to "reproduce and Share the Licensed Material, in whole or in part, for NonCommercial purposes only". ShareAlike applies to adapted licensed material, not automatically to unrelated Korri code.

The [CC BY-NC 4.0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/legalcode.en) imposes noncommercial use and attribution duties without ShareAlike. This is the asset license on Mot's 8-Ball Pool.

Both licenses define NonCommercial as "not primarily intended for or directed towards commercial advantage or monetary compensation". Korri's stated noncommercial scope fits the intended candidate search. Third parties who sell devices with these games still need to satisfy that restriction or obtain separate permission.

The [MIT text](https://opensource.org/license/mit) permits distribution and sale, subject to retaining its copyright and permission notice. Preserve the actual grant and complete credits for each selected version. Keep the licensing of bundled games separate from Korri's own license.

## Packaging and runtime limits

The itch.io PICO-8 source release of Mot's Grand Prix uses multiple cartridges. Preserve the full set and test it with the selected runtime. Do not mistake a single menu cartridge for an entire multi-cartridge game.

Birds With Guns documents a buttons-only mode as well as mouse controls. Test the buttons-only mode on a handheld before selecting it for a starter bundle.

A cartridge license does not grant redistribution permission for the full official PICO-8 application. Its actual application license was not obtained in this research. [Lexaloffle's FAQ](https://www.lexaloffle.com/pico-8.php?page=faq) allows exported games with contributing authors' permission; an exported runtime is not the full editor/player application.

[FAKE-08's developer README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jtothebell/fake-08/master/README.md) identifies an MIT license plus dependency licenses and warns about incomplete compatibility. An open runtime is a possible separate route, not proof that these games run correctly on Korri. No runtime was selected, built, or deployed.
