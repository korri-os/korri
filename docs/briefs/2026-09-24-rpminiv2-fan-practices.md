# Retroid Pocket Mini V2 fan control: what others ship (research begun 2026-09-24)

**Answer:** ROCKNIX now has a specific SM8250 temperature-driven fan policy, but it landed **after** Korri's pinned September 1 kernel source. Its September 17 change adds a kernel fan cooling map to the Retroid shared board tree, three selectable threshold profiles, a userspace fallback, and a proposed tachometer input. The author tested it on the **Pocket 5**, not the Mini V2. It is evidence for a possible Mini V2 design, not proof of Mini V2 fan rotation or a validated Mini V2 temperature curve. I found no verified stock Android Mini V2 fan policy.

## Hardware and implementation limits

- The **pinned** Retroid shared DTSI declares the fan on PM8150L LPG channel 3 with a 100,000 ns period, 5 V fan supply, and cooling values `0 32 64 128 255`. It also declares skin, PCB, Wi-Fi, XO and connector PMIC thermal zones with trips; none binds the fan to a trip in that file. The Mini V2 board inherits this shared declaration. Sources: [ROCKNIX pinned Retroid DTSI](https://github.com/ROCKNIX/distribution/blob/e81d1fc943458fb13cffe1646761e9452b29ddc1/projects/ROCKNIX/devices/SM8250/linux/dts/qcom/sm8250-retroidpocket-common.dtsi), [Linux PWM fan binding](https://www.kernel.org/doc/Documentation/devicetree/bindings/hwmon/pwm-fan.yaml), [Linux thermal-zone binding](https://www.kernel.org/doc/Documentation/devicetree/bindings/thermal/thermal-zones.yaml).
- The **new** ROCKNIX patch adds an IRQ on **TLMM GPIO 0**, `pulses-per-revolution = <2>`, and a `fan-tach-state` pin on the shared Retroid board. Its discovery script selects `fan1_input` for RPM when present and otherwise presents commanded PWM duty. The patch therefore proposes a tach path that our pinned DTSI lacks. **The author only reports Pocket 5 testing**; an IRQ declaration and an RPM sysfs node do not establish that the Mini V2 actually sends tach pulses. Source: [PR #3339 patch](https://github.com/ROCKNIX/distribution/pull/3339.patch), [PR test statement](https://github.com/ROCKNIX/distribution/pull/3339).
- ROCKNIX's 2025 [Pocket 5 and original Mini fan-ramping fix](https://github.com/ROCKNIX/distribution/releases/tag/20250115) predates Mini V2 support. Do not use that older release note as evidence of a V2 curve.
- Korri's [Odin 2 Portal fan curve](../../nix/devices/odin2portal/platform-policy.nix) is measured on **SM8550/Thor-class hardware**, not this SM8250 Mini V2. Its stall PWM and temperatures must not be copied as Mini V2 calibration. The generic [Korri fan-control module](../../nix/base/fan-control.nix) exists but is not enabled by `nix/devices/rpminiv2/`. Enabling it would add a separate controller; combining it with ROCKNIX's kernel mapping would require explicit ownership to avoid fighting over PWM.

## What this establishes for Korri

The most relevant public design to **evaluate**, not copy wholesale, is the merged SM8250 ROCKNIX PR: kernel-mapped `cluster1-thermal` active cooling with a chosen profile, plus a separately owned fallback when no map exists. It also offers a specific tach GPIO to investigate. This would replace Korri's fixed 70 startup-only behavior. It changes kernel/device-tree and runtime thermal ownership, and would require a new image and a short owner-approved device validation. The cost is a more complex policy that can fail or interfere with existing thermal behavior; it does not solve the observed Mini V2 PMIC `temp` read errors, prove rotation, or provide a safe sustained-load baseline. **Do not flash, boot, change trips, or assume these numbers are validated on this Mini V2 from this research alone.**
