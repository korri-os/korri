Recovery:
- Chromium repeatedly exited with SIGSEGV in exploration. The first recovery
  used one reboot with the same generation. Final baselines ran after it.
- After the final native measurement, the native unit stopped successfully,
  but starting Chromium failed again with SIGSEGV/private pipe closure.
- Confirmed zero zone-spike units and no native executable running; the
  runtime directory was absent. Stopped the kiosk, restarted the unchanged
  korri-compositor.service, and started the kiosk. It became active at once
  with MainPID=9065 and NRestarts=0. A second reboot was not needed.
- The immediate failure was Chromium SIGSEGV. The underlying crash cause is
  unknown. A stale compositor/client interaction is an inference, not proven.
  Similar crashes had occurred before the first successful native window,
  so this experiment does not establish that Slint caused them.
