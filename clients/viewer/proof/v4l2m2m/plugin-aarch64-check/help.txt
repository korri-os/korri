korri-viewer 0.1.0 (GPL-3.0-or-later)
Usage: korri-viewer stream|pair [Moonlight arguments] HOST
PC picture, sound and input. -viewonly disables input device discovery; the window's own keyboard and mouse still forward, as in Moonlight.
Handheld picture: -platform v4l2request or -platform v4l2m2m, with -input <device> up to 6 times.
